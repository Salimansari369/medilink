Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Most appointments are confirmed within 2 minutes. Same-day slots are available for most specialists in major cities.

Is MediLinks free to use?

Platform use is completely free. You only pay the doctor's consultation fee, which is shown upfront before booking.

Are the doctors verified?

Yes. Every doctor goes through a 7-step verification including credential checks, registration validation, and ongoing patient feedback reviews.
